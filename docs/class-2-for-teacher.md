# Class Exercise 2: four tunes for the teacher to confirm
# แบบฝึกหัดในห้อง 2: สี่ประโยคที่ขอให้อาจารย์ยืนยันทำนอง

Melobi turned the in-class sheet (14 items, split at each `//` into 25 sentences) into practice sentences. The sheet has no
answer key, so every sentence follows the course rules. Four needed a judgement call. Please say which reading you teach.

Melobi นำใบงานในห้อง (14 ข้อ แยกที่เครื่องหมาย `//` เป็น 25 ประโยค) มาทำเป็นแบบฝึกหัด ใบงานไม่มีเฉลย
จึงใช้กฎที่เรียนในห้องกับทุกประโยค มี 4 ประโยคที่ต้องตัดสินใจเอง ขอให้อาจารย์บอกว่าสอนแบบไหน

Notation: 2-3-1 = rises to the peak, then falls to low. 2-3 = rises and stays up. 2-3-2 = rises, then comes back to mid (not finished).
3-1 = starts high and falls. `|` separates thought groups.

| # | Sentence | What Melobi uses now | The other possible reading |
|---|----------|----------------------|----------------------------|
| 1 | **Life isn't easy, is it?** (item 9) | Speaker is **sure**: `Life isn't easy` 2-3-1 + tag `is it` **3-1** (falls) | Speaker is really asking: tag **2-3** (rises) |
| 2 | **Which one would you like, coffee or tea?** (item 10) | `Which one would you like,` **2-3-2** (open) + `coffee` **2-3** + `or tea?` **2-3** (both choices rise, the last too) | `Which one would you like` 2-3-1 (falls) + `coffee` 2-3 + `or tea` 2-3-1 (last choice falls) |
| 3 | **Pick it up, please.** (item 13) | One group, **2-3-1**, peak on `please` | Two groups: `Pick it UP,` 2-3-2 + `PLEASE.` 2-3-1 |
| 4 | **Mr. President, can I talk to you please?** (item 7) | `Mr. PRESIDENT,` **2-3** + `can I talk to you PLEASE?` **2-3**, peak on `please` | Peak on `talk`: `can I TALK to you please?` |

Everything else follows the course rules directly: statements, wh-questions and commands fall (2-3-1); yes/no questions, names and
every item of a list or choice rise (2-3), except that the last item of a statement list falls; a part that is not finished
comes back to mid (2-3-2).
